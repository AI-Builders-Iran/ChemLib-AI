Party One (Client): Novin Afzar Pars Co. (Private Joint Stock Company)

Party Two (Contractor): Arya Smart Technology Institute

Subject of the Agreement:
Design, development, and deployment of an AI-based document management
system for automatically extracting information from invoices and
contracts belonging to the Client, including analysis, implementation,
testing, and technical support for a period of six months.

Term of the Agreement:
Start Date: October 1, 2026
End Date: March 21, 2027

Total Contract Value:
The total value of this Agreement is USD 20,000 (twenty thousand US
dollars), payable in four quarterly installments.

Payment Terms:
- First installment: 25% of the total amount, upon signing the Agreement
- Second installment: 25% of the total amount, upon delivery of the MVP
- Third installment: 25% of the total amount, upon completion of user
  acceptance testing (UAT)
- Fourth installment: remaining 25%, upon final delivery and deployment

Termination Clause:
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days' prior
written notice. Upon termination, the Contractor shall deliver all code
and documentation produced up to the termination date to the Client.

Confidentiality Clause:
Both parties agree to treat all technical, financial, and business
information exchanged during the performance of this Agreement as
confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party without the
prior written consent of the other party. This obligation shall survive
for two years after the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

Governing Law:
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Islamic Republic of
Iran. Any disputes arising from this Agreement shall be subject to the
exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of Tehran.
